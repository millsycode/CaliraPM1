## Product: Infinity X Core Management Software

Positioning: "core facility management" platform for research institutions — heavier emphasis on multi-core, multi-facility institutional administration than on booking/scheduling alone. Available as SaaS or on-premise.

### Full module list (from [all-modules](https://ideaelan.com/all-modules/) and [product page](https://ideaelan.com/infinity-x-core-management-software/))

1. **Mobile App** — iOS/Android, instant reservations, ops dashboard, incident reporting with photos, in-app chat/notifications
2. **Instrument Scheduler** — two-click reservations, bi-directional Outlook/Google calendar sync, rule enforcement, waitlists, automated billing calc
3. **Actual Usage Tracker™** — real-time usage tracking for both computer-controlled and non-networked equipment, anomaly detection, revenue forecasting
4. **Cloud Storage** — up to 5TB, access controls, audit logging, search
5. **Service Request / Sample Submission** — 300+ prebuilt workflow templates, milestone-based pricing, ELN-style capabilities
6. **Billing and Invoicing** — approval workflows, audit trails, PI/group budget monitoring, ERP integration
7. **Publication Tracking** — pulls from publication repositories via ORCID etc., ties research output back to facility usage (accreditation/impact reporting angle)
8. **Project Management** — multi-PI, multi-facility, multi-currency budgeting
9. **Business Intelligence/Reporting** — 1,000+ report templates, Excel/PDF/Word export
10. **Themes and Branding** — white-label look per institution
11. **Supplies and Inventory Tracking**
12. **Notifications** — email/SMS, role-based
13. **Sample Management** — barcode + well-plate templates, LIMS integration
14. **Stockroom** — barcode-driven, vendor management
15. **Web Wizard** — guided setup/configuration
16. **Technical Issue Tracking & Analytics** — uptime/downtime, preventive maintenance data
17. **Asset Management** — "market leader since 2010" self-claim, contract management, maintenance audit trails
18. **Announcement Module**
19. **Infinity X AI Module** — see below
20. **Infinity X Network Module** — a searchable global network (cross-institution equipment discovery — worth watching, not many competitors have this)
21. **Workflow Management** — custom approval chains
22. Plus: Training module, Safety Inspection templates, Integrated Support

### Infinity X AI

Layered across all four user roles (end user, core admin, financial admin, institutional admin). Notable: they say it's built on **multiple third-party LLMs** — ChatGPT 4 Pro, ChatGPT 4o, LLAMA 3.2, and **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** — rather than a single proprietary model. Marketing framing is generic ("your imagination is the limit," "launch into the digital orbit in a few weeks") — no concrete AI use-case documentation found beyond the module description. — [ideaelan.com/infinityai](https://ideaelan.com/infinityai/)

### Pricing / packaging

Four tiers: **Lite, X (standard), Ultimate, Institution-wide** — differentiated by capacity limits (# instruments, facilities, labs, users, templates: limited on Lite/X, unlimited on Ultimate/Institution-wide) and by which advanced modules are included (AI, inventory, barcode scanner, stockroom, sample management, branding, training, workflow automation, usage tracker are gated to higher tiers). Core scheduling/billing/reporting modules are included at every tier. No public dollar figures — SaaS is described as "low initial cost" subscription (covers software/upgrades/hosting/backup); on-premise has higher upfront cost, lower ongoing cost. Contact-sales model, consistent with the rest of this competitive set (Q.Reserve is still the only vendor in Calira's tracked list with published dollar pricing). — [compare-features](https://ideaelan.com/compare-features/)

Note: I also fetched `ideaelan.com/pricing/` directly and it returned unrelated generic SaaS boilerplate pricing ($19/$29/$59 "Personal/Startup/Business" workspace & e-commerce plans, plus a vague "Enterprise" tier). That doesn't match anything else about this product and looks like stale/placeholder theme content rather than real Infinity X pricing — disregard it, it's not reflected in the table above.

## Customers

47 named institutions on their [Featured Customers](https://ideaelan.com/featured-customers/) page — no attributed testimonial quotes, just logos. Skews heavily academic/institutional (universities, medical centers, national labs) across US, Canada, UK, Europe, Middle East, Australia — global footprint, "20+ countries" per their own claim. Notable names: Caltech, Scripps Research, Columbia, McGill, UCL, KAUST, Regeneron (one of very few industry/pharma names on the list — most of the roster is academic), SABIC.

This is important for how you frame Infinity X against Calira: **their customer base is overwhelmingly university/academic core facilities, not biotech/pharma industry R&D** — which is explicitly outside Calira's ICP per [personas.md](personas.md) but confirms this vendor is optimized for a different buyer (university core facility director managing cost-recovery billing across many PIs) than Calira's industry LabOps manager. The "multi-PI billing/cost-recovery" emphasis throughout their module set (budget monitoring per PI, ERP integration, publication tracking for grant/accreditation purposes) is a university-specific pattern, less relevant to industry labs.

## Read for Calira positioning

- **Not a direct ICP overlap today** — their customer list is almost entirely academic core facilities, not industry biotech/pharma. But two things worth watching: (1) they explicitly market "institution-wide" and multi-site/multi-currency capability, so they could credibly move upmarket or into larger pharma if they chose to; (2) Regeneron and SABIC on their customer list prove they *can* land industry accounts even if it's not their center of gravity.
- **Their strength is university cost-recovery/billing complexity** (multi-PI budgets, ERP integration, publication/grant tracking) — a genuinely different problem shape than Calira's "reduce conflicts / improve visibility / prevent downtime" value themes for industry LabOps. Not much overlap to fight over head-to-head.
- **Weak public marketing/proof surface** — no reviews on G2/Capterra, no case studies, no attributed testimonials, thin screenshot presence, and one broken/placeholder pricing page. For a 2010-founded, bootstrapped company this reads as under-invested marketing rather than a stealthy strong competitor — consistent with the broader pattern across this whole competitive category (see [competitor-updates-2026-07.md](competitor-updates-2026-07.md) headline takeaways: "public web sources are thin for this category by nature").
- **Founder's domain credibility (ex-Zeiss/Bio-Rad microscopy specialist) is a real asset** in university core-facility sales conversations — that's a story Calira doesn't have and can't easily counter with messaging; it's more relevant in academic deals than industry ones, which is Calira's actual ICP.
