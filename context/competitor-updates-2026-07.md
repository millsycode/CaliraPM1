# Competitor product/pricing update scan — July 2026

Research window: roughly January–July 2026 (last 6 months). Compiled from web research across all 12 tracked competitors. Confidence is uneven — this category (core-facility/lab-equipment scheduling software) mostly sells to institutional buyers and doesn't run much public-facing PR, so several vendors show little to no dated public activity in this window. Flagged accordingly per company. Treat unverified/undated items as directional, not confirmed.

---

## Headline takeaways

- **AI is the dominant theme this half**, but only at the two vendors with VC backing: **Cenevo (Labguru)** shipped an AI Protocol Conversion Agent and a no-code AI Automation Agent (Feb 2026), and **Skedda** has an "AI-Driven Insights Agent" (chat-based analytics) on its public roadmap for later 2026.
- **Labguru is no longer a standalone brand.** It rebranded to **Cenevo** (with sister product Mosaic, formerly Titian) in mid-2025, backed by Battery Ventures. Product tracking should shift to "Cenevo" going forward.
- **Optima (LRMS) and Calpendo both shipped substantive scheduling releases** in April–May 2026 — the closest direct feature-for-feature competition to Calira's core booking/scheduling use case. Optima's v2026.2 added cross-departmental scheduling, workload balancing, and multi-instrument/formula-based booking. Calpendo's v12.0 added 2FA, mobile support, and a CTMS speed upgrade.
- **Optima also picked up ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certifications** (March 2026) — relevant to Calira's "compliance/audit readiness" category entry point; worth watching as a competitive proof point in enterprise deals.
- **Q.Reserve remains the only competitor with fully public, itemized pricing** ($3,000–$10,000+/yr tiers plus nonprofit/education discount programs). Everyone else in the set is quote-only.
- **Skedda had the most active, dated release cadence** of any vendor researched — near-monthly product roundups (Feb, May, Jun 2026) covering booking approvals, visitor management, Zoom/Webex/Outlook integrations, and check-in analytics. Note Skedda's public pricing ($99–$199/mo) is for its **AllBooked** consumer/community sub-brand, not necessarily the Workplace product comparable to Calira.
- **Weakest/least active signal:** ULab — its ulab.io domain has lapsed and is now parked for sale (live product site is ulabequipment.com, no dated content found at all). Bookit-lab shows no substantive public activity beyond a routine software build number. Agilent iLab's own release-notes page shows nothing dated past 2024 despite several undated "new feature" marketing pages.

## Labguru (now Cenevo)

**Context:** Labguru and Titian Software (Mosaic) jointly rebranded as **Cenevo** in July 2025, backed by Battery Ventures. Labguru is now one of two Cenevo product lines. Track "Cenevo" going forward.

**New capabilities:**
- Feb 10, 2026 — Two new AI agents: an **AI Protocol Conversion Agent** (turns legacy/paper protocols into structured digital protocols) and an **AI Automation Agent** (plain-language, no-code automation setup). Demoed at SLAS 2026.
- Apr 9, 2026 — **Labguru Customer Portal**, purpose-built for CRO/CDMO client collaboration into lab data/status.
- May 20, 2026 — AI-powered natural-language inventory search, but shipped on sister product **Mosaic**, not Labguru directly — same AI push, adjacent product.

**New service offerings:** Co-marketing partnership with HighRes (Cellario OS) around "agentic connected lab" integration (Jun 15, 2026 — Mosaic-centric). Second annual AI-adoption survey published (Jun 18) — only ~5% of labs using AI agents in production, per their own data.

**Pricing:** No public pricing; fully quote-based. No evidence of a structural change.

## Calpendo (Exprodo)

**New capabilities:** Version 12.0 (first released Apr 2026, updated May 2026) — two-factor authentication, mobile support, text-editor improvements, upload virus scanning, and a notable performance upgrade to the Clinical Trials Management System (CTMS) module for bulk automatic bookings. Rolling release notes also list (undated, likely recent) unified search/calendar booking display, instrument photos in the calendar, free-text cancellation reasons, custom calendar-template messages, and infinite-scroll calendar views.

**New service offerings:** **Calpendo Enterprise** — federated multi-site architecture with central governance and aggregated reporting across facility "shards." Launch date unconfirmed, may predate the window.

**Pricing:** Starter vs. Premium tiers (Premium unlocks full automation/workflow engine) plus a custom Enterprise tier — all quote-based. No evidence of a pricing structure change.

## Optima (Optima LRMS)

**New capabilities:** Version 2026.2 (released May 4, 2026, announced via press release May 7 — notable because Optima normally ships quietly without formal PR, suggesting they saw this as a bigger release). Added: cross-departmental scheduling (extending booking to QA, Engineering, external consultants, inter-lab sharing), workload balancing by staff seniority, multi-instrument/formula-based booking tied to sample volume or technique, dynamic/emergency rescheduling (sick staff, breakdowns, urgent reanalysis), customer/project-dedicated resource assignment with FTE caps, and expanded forecasting/reporting.
